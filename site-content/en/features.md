# What can you do at Chùa Mây?

Chùa Mây offers five simple online activities: incense, a respectful bow, sitting still, moving beads, and a wooden-block sound. Each is only an interaction in the web space.

## Five activities

- **Incense:** view an illustrated burner; it is not a required ritual.
- **A respectful bow:** bow toward a neutral silhouette without promising an outcome.
- **Sitting still:** leave the screen quiet for a few minutes if you wish.
- **Moving beads:** tap to move beads in an illustrated strand.
- **Wooden-block sound:** play a sample sound with stop and volume controls.
