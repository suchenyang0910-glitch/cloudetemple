The website does not require login, has no form, does not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers, and does not place advertising.

[待律师确认] [待母语审校] The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits, including which pages are opened and broad distributions by device type and region. According to Cloudflare’s public description, the product does not use cookies, perform cross-site tracking, or build personal profiles.
