## Buddhist Era

Under the common Vietnamese convention, from a year's Vesak day the Buddhist Era is that Gregorian year plus 544; before Vesak it is that Gregorian year plus 543. Thus Vesak 2027 ({{date:15/4}}) begins Buddhist Era 2571, while the period before it is 2570. This is a calendar convention, not a doctrinal claim.

Temples and traditions may place some days differently; for example, Vesak may be observed on the 8th day or the 15th day of the fourth lunar month. This page keeps the list above as a neutral reference.
