# 2027 lunar calendar

This overview lists the first day and full moon of all 12 lunar months in 2027. Dates are calculated in Vietnam time (UTC+7) with the same converter used for every calendar cell; the result follows the commonly used Vietnamese lunar calendar.

Tết Nguyên Đán 2027 begins on the first day of the first lunar month, 06/02/2027 (Saturday).
