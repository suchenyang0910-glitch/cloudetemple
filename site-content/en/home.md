## Five activities

- **Incense:** tap to light an illustrated stick.
- **A respectful bow:** bow toward a faceless silhouette with no held object.
- **Sitting still:** leave the screen quiet for a few minutes if you wish.
- **Moving beads:** tap each bead in a steady rhythm; there are no points or levels.
- **Wooden-block sound:** play one sample sound, adjust the volume, or mute it.
