Chùa Mây (Cloud Temple) is a quiet online space to visit, sit still, and read the 2027 lunar calendar. Read Vietnamese or English, open the experience when convenient, and leave whenever you wish.

Chùa Mây is an online experience, not a physical temple. Last updated: 2026-11-13.

## What can you do?

- **Incense:** tap to light a single illustrated stick and watch the small glow appear slowly.
- **A respectful bow:** bow toward a simple silhouette, like pausing for a moment before moving on.
- **Sitting still:** leave the screen quiet for a few minutes and return when you are ready.
- **Moving beads:** tap each bead and follow the steady rhythm without turning it into a score.
- **Wooden-block sound:** try one sample sound, adjust the volume, or turn it off whenever you wish.

## 2027 lunar calendar

The [2027 lunar calendar](/en/lunar-calendar-2027/) has an overview and 12 pages grouped by Gregorian month. Each day lists its Gregorian date, weekday, lunar day, and lunar month; first-day and full-moon rows are bold and have a downloadable image.

## Three common questions

- **What is Chùa Mây?** An online space, not a physical temple.
- **Do I need to pay or register?** No registration is needed; there is no charge at present.
- **When is Tết Nguyên Đán 2027?** The first day of the first lunar month is 06/02/2027 (Saturday, UTC+7).
