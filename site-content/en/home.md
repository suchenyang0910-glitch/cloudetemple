# Chùa Mây (Cloud Temple)

Chùa Mây (Cloud Temple) is a quiet online space to visit, sit still, and read the 2027 lunar calendar. Open it on the web or in Telegram, read Vietnamese or English, and leave without turning the experience into a promise or commitment.

## Actions

- [Mở trên web](https://h5.chuamay.org/)
- [Mở trong Telegram](https://t.me/ChuaMayBot)

Chùa Mây is an online experience, not a physical temple. Last updated: 2026-11-13.

## What can you do?

You can view an illustrated burner, bow toward a neutral silhouette, sit still, move beads, or play a wooden-block sample. These are five web interactions with stop and volume controls where relevant; they are not presented as promises or required rituals.

## 2027 lunar calendar

The [2027 lunar calendar](/en/lunar-calendar-2027/) has an overview and 12 pages grouped by Gregorian month. Each day lists its Gregorian date, weekday, lunar day, and lunar month; first-day and full-moon rows are bold and have a downloadable image.

## Three common questions

- **What is Chùa Mây?** An online space, not a physical temple.
- **Do I need to pay or register?** This introductory experience does not require either.
- **When is Tết Nguyên Đán 2027?** The first day of the first lunar month is 06/02/2027 (Saturday, UTC+7).
