# Frequently asked questions

SEO title: Chùa Mây frequently asked questions
Meta description: Read short answers about Chùa Mây, the 2027 calendar, Telegram access, and privacy.

This page gives visitors short answers about Chùa Mây, the 2027 calendar, and how to ask about data. Last updated: 2026-11-13.

## What is Chùa Mây?

Chùa Mây is a quiet online space to visit, read a calendar, and try a few simple interactions.

## Is Chùa Mây a real temple?

No. Chùa Mây is not a physical place.

## Does Chùa Mây represent a church or religious body?

No. The introductory site does not represent a church or religious body.

## Do I have to pay?

The introductory experience does not require payment.

## Do I have to register?

There is no registration requirement for the introductory content or calendar.

## How do I open Chùa Mây in Telegram?

Open [t.me/ChuaMayBot](https://t.me/ChuaMayBot) on a device with Telegram.

## Can I use Chùa Mây on a phone?

Yes. The pages are written for small screens; use a modern browser or Telegram.

## When is Tết Nguyên Đán 2027?

The first day of the first lunar month is Saturday, 06/02/2027 (UTC+7).

## When is the first full moon of 2027?

The full moon of the first lunar month is Saturday, 20/02/2027 (UTC+7).

## What are mùng 1 and rằm?

In these tables, mùng 1 is the first day and rằm is day 15 of a lunar month.

## How do I download a calendar image?

Open a Gregorian-month page and select the calendar image link below its table.

## Where does the calendar data come from?

The dates are generated by the calendar converter used for these pages in Vietnam time (UTC+7), with 2027 boundary checks.

## Does Chùa Mây collect my information?

Only the minimum data needed to load pages, process an interaction, or answer support is used; see [Privacy](/en/privacy/).

## When will the pet memorial garden open?

There is no opening date. The page is marked **Sắp mở / Coming soon** and only introduces the idea.

## Does Chùa Mây promise a spiritual result?

No. The content is neutral and does not sell or promise efficacy.

## Where can I ask about my data?

Ask through the Telegram link on the Privacy page.
