# About Chùa Mây

SEO title: What is Chùa Mây?
Meta description: Learn what Chùa Mây is, what it is not, and what the online space represents.

Chùa Mây is an online space to visit, sit still, and read a calendar. It is not a physical temple, does not represent a church or religious body, and does not sell claims of spiritual results.
