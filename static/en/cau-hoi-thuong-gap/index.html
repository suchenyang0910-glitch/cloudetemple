Cloud Temple frequently asked questions

Cloud Temple is a website and Telegram Mini App for a few quiet minutes of incense, bowing, sitting, wooden fish and prayer beads in a hand-painted temple. It does not replace visiting a temple or family rites. No account, no fees, and no donations of any kind. The temple is an illustration, not a real temple, and Cloud Temple does not represent any religious organisation. Counts and preferences stay on your device; there are no rankings. If you hear nothing, check the speaker button and your phone's silent mode; iPhone browsers do not vibrate, Telegram and Android do. Open it in Telegram at t.me/ChuaMayBot. A family memorial with lunar death-anniversary reminders is coming; you can leave an email to be notified.
