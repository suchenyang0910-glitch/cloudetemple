How to use 108 prayer beads

108 beads are often linked to the 108 afflictions; shorter malas of 54, 27, 21 or 18 also exist. The guru bead is the largest bead, usually with a tassel; some malas add a marker bead every 27. Hold the mala, start next to the guru bead and pull one bead toward you with the thumb for each recitation or breath. When you reach the guru bead, a common custom is not to pass over it but to turn the mala and continue in the other direction. In Cloud Temple you swipe down or tap to move one bead, with a soft click, a light bowl sound at marker beads and a bell at the guru bead.
