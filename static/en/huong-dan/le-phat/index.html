How to bow to the Buddha

A full prostration brings five points to the floor: both knees, both elbows and the forehead. Three bows are commonly understood as honouring the Buddha, the Dharma and the Sangha; 108 bows are often linked to the 108 afflictions and are used in repentance practice. Go slowly with your breath. Beginners can start with 27 or 54, and anyone with knee or back pain, or who is pregnant, can simply bow the head. In Cloud Temple you choose 3, 27, 54 or 108 bows, about four seconds each, marked by a soft bowl sound.
