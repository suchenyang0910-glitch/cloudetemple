How to light incense respectfully

Most people light one or three sticks; three are often understood as honouring the Buddha, the Dharma and the Sangha. Wash your hands, light the incense, wave the flame out by hand rather than blowing it, raise the sticks to your forehead with both hands and bow three times, then place them upright in the burner. Customs differ by family and temple, so follow local guidance. If you cannot visit a temple, Cloud Temple offers a one-minute quiet moment; it does not replace a real visit.
