Chùa Mây Tiếng Việt

A five-minute sit for beginners

Sit on a cushion or chair with a straight but relaxed back, hands resting in your lap, eyes closed or lowered. Breathe naturally and count each breath from one to ten, then start again. When your mind wanders, gently return to one; returning is the practice. Finish by opening your eyes slowly and feeling your feet on the ground. This is not a substitute for medical or psychological care. In Cloud Temple you choose 1, 3, 5 or 10 minutes with silence, forest or rain sounds, and a bell marks the end.
